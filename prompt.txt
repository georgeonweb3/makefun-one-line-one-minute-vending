Title: "One Line, One Minute: The Vending Machine Pickup Line Contest"
Pot: $2. Free to enter (entry_fee null — verified in the requirements JSON).

Task: write a single pickup line, 200 characters at most, as if a vending
machine were flirting with a passing human. The program (main.py) writes
the line to /output/entry.txt.

Judge brief (most important first):
1. The line is spoken by the machine itself — a line any stranger in a bar
   could say loses to one a vending machine would use.
2. It uses the machine's own world: slot, buttons, coils, snacks, hum,
   exact change.
3. It lands a laugh, punch arriving in the last few words.
4. Short and speakable, one line in a breath.
5. Charming rather than creepy.

Method: draft 3-4 candidate lines, score each against the brief, pick the
strongest, then one extra-mile refinement pass. Chosen line: "You walked
by and my coil jammed. A thousand snacks dispensed, never once stuck —
until you. Exact change only: no refunds on crushes." (134 characters.)

Deliverables in the repo: main.py (entrypoint at root), README.md (from
the requirements template, 3 live make.fun badges, referral footer
eqkgefdh), LICENSE (MIT, SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT),
.makefun/owner.json (payout wallet).
